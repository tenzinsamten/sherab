# PRD Quality Review — PRD: Parent Role — tib-class

## Overall verdict
This is a focused, honest PRD for an internal tool. The Vision is specific and earned (about 40 sessions a year, a teacher-meeting conclusion). The read-only stance is stated as a decision, and the FRs mostly carry testable consequences. It is at risk in two places. First, it is written as if the school had no students yet: it never says what happens to the students already approved under v1, who have no Parent and, under FR-8, lose the ability to set leave at all. Second, §4.3 bundles a stricter streak-leave policy that the thesis and metrics do not justify, and its state model and edge cases are under-specified. Settle both before story creation. The rest can be fixed with light edits.

## Decision-readiness — adequate
The big calls are stated as decisions, not as considerations: parent-first registration (§4.1 "Parents come first"), read-only access with exactly two actions (§1 "A parent can take exactly two actions"), leave moves from the Student to the Parent (§4.3), and one Parent account per family (§6). §5 "Changes to the Existing Spec" lists which contract clauses are overridden, which a maintainer can act on.

What is missing is the cost side of these decisions. Parent-first registration means a new family cannot get a child into class until one Admin approves the Parent ("never more than a week", UJ-1). That makes a single Admin the gate on every new enrolment, and the PRD presents this as a service level, not a trade-off. Taking the leave control away from Students gives up older students' autonomy and assumes the Parent is always engaged. That is not acknowledged. The stricter streak rules (FR-9/FR-10) make streaks harder to keep for every child, which works against CAP-4's motivation intent, and that is not discussed. There are no `[NOTE FOR PM]` callouts anywhere, although these tensions are real.

### Findings
- **high** Trade-offs of the parent-first and leave-transfer decisions are not named (§1, §4.1, §4.3) — The PRD states what was chosen but not what was given up: a Admin bottleneck on every new enrolment, loss of student agency over their own leave, and harder-to-keep streaks for all children. A reader who objects on any of these finds nothing to engage with. *Fix:* Add a short "Trade-offs" paragraph, or `[NOTE FOR PM]` callouts, at §4.1 and §4.3 naming each cost and why it is accepted.
- **medium** Open Question 1 is a release blocker with no owner (§10 Q1) — "Must be decided before real data is stored" means the feature cannot ship until it is resolved, but no owner or deadline is given, and §7 MVP Scope does not list it as a gate. *Fix:* Name an owner and move it into §7 as an explicit precondition for go-live.

## Substance over theater — strong
There is little furniture here. There is one persona (Parent), and it drives decisions ("Non-technical; uses a family phone" → read-only, simple landing card). The Vision could not be swapped into another PRD: "The school meets roughly 40 times a year" and "discovering the gap on Sunday" are specific. The counter-metric SM-C1 ("pressure to tick boxes rather than practise") is a real insight, not template filler. There is no innovation or differentiation section, which is correct for this tool.

### Findings
- **low** One adjective NFR (§8) — "Must stay simple enough for non-technical parents without instructions" has no bound. *Fix:* Tie it to something checkable, e.g. "a first-time Parent reaches a Linked Child's overdue Homework from login in ≤2 taps with no help text", or drop it as covered by the existing SPEC constraint.

## Strategic coherence — adequate
The thesis is clear and good: parents who can see homework status mid-week will get children to do more practice between the ~40 sessions (§1). §4.1, §4.2 and SM-1 follow directly from it, and SM-C1 is a real counter-metric.

§4.3 is a second, separate policy change riding along with it. The Streak becomes stricter ("Any other leave … consumes one grace week"), with a new admin setting and teacher approval of Sick Leave. Moving leave-setting to the Parent can be defended under the thesis ("so teachers … know who will be in class"). Making the streak stricter cannot: nothing in §1 or §2.1 explains why short-notice leave should now cost grace. No metric validates it either. The JTBD "Tell the teacher ahead of time" has no SM, and none of SM-1–SM-3 covers leave. This is the part of the PRD that reads like "something someone also wanted".

### Findings
- **high** The stricter streak-leave rules are not grounded in the thesis or measured (§4.3 FR-9/FR-10, §9) — The Planned / Short-Notice / Sick classification changes CAP-4 for every student, but the Vision never states the problem it solves, and no metric shows whether it works. *Fix:* Either state the rationale in §1 (e.g. "late cancellations disrupt teachers' planning") and add a metric such as "share of leave set as Planned Leave", or split FR-9/FR-10 into a follow-up increment and keep this PRD to moving leave to the Parent.
- **medium** SM-1 has no baseline method and no target (§9 SM-1) — "compared with the term before … Target: an increase" with about 20 sessions a term and a handful of students per class will be dominated by noise and by teacher changes. *Fix:* Define the baseline window, state what counts as "Done by due date" (Done timestamp ≤ due date), and set a minimum meaningful change, even a rough one (e.g. +10 percentage points).

## Done-ness clarity — adequate
FR-1–FR-7, FR-11 and FR-12 are well built. Each has concrete, testable consequences ("A Parent Email already used by another Parent account is refused", "A rejected Student no longer appears", "No control on the page lets the Parent change Homework…"). §4.3 is where an engineer will get stuck: the leave state model and several edge rules are either inconsistent or missing.

### Findings
- **high** The Sick state is inconsistent across Glossary, FR-8 and FR-10 (§3 "Session Leave", §4.3 description, FR-8, FR-10) — The Glossary defines Session Leave as "Coming / On leave", FR-8 lets the Parent set "Coming or On leave", and the §4.3 description says "Coming, On leave, or Sick". FR-8 allows changes "until the Session starts", while FR-10 allows Sick "until the end of the day after it". An engineer cannot tell whether Sick is a third state or a flag on On leave, or which deadline applies. *Fix:* Define the state set once in the Glossary (e.g. Coming / On leave / Sick / not answered, with Sick carrying pending/approved/rejected) and give FR-8 and FR-10 one consistent editing window per state.
- **high** Planned Leave classification is ambiguous under edits and schedule changes (FR-9) — "classifies On leave … by when it was set" does not say what happens if the Parent sets On leave 3 weeks ahead, flips to Coming, then back to On leave 1 week ahead. It also does not cover a teacher moving the session's start time, an extra session added with less than 2 weeks' notice (CAP-10), or class days that the Admin has not marked 2 weeks out, which makes Planned Leave impossible. *Fix:* Add consequences: the timestamp is the latest change to On leave; the notice is measured against the session's start as it stood when the leave was set; and sessions created inside the Leave Notice Period treat any leave set before start as Planned.
- **medium** Pending Sick Leave has no end state (FR-10) — "While pending, Sick Leave does not yet protect the Streak" gives no rule for a Teacher who never decides. The Teacher-side surface (where the request appears, and whether it is part of the existing approval queue) is not specified, and §5 does not list the Teacher-facing change. *Fix:* Add the Teacher queue location and a timeout rule (e.g. undecided after N days counts as approved or rejected), and list it under §5 (CAP-2 or CAP-11).
- **medium** "Open" vs "pending" Homework is undefined and clashes with account "Pending" (§3 Child Summary, FR-6, FR-7) — The Glossary says the Child Summary shows "pending and overdue" counts, FR-6 says "open", and "Pending" is already the account-state term. It is also unclear whether "open" counts items beyond the look-ahead window. *Fix:* Use "Open Homework" everywhere, define it in the Glossary (not Done, not archived, within the look-ahead window), and keep "Pending" for accounts only.
- **medium** FR-7 "teacher-side label should say so" is a soft requirement about existing data (FR-7) — Existing skill-status notes (`notes` in `0003_roster_skill_tracking.sql`) were written on the understanding that only teachers would read them. The FR exposes the full history to Parents and only adds "should" wording to a label. *Fix:* Make it testable (the notes field shows "Visible to the student's parent"), and decide whether notes written before the release are shown, hidden, or reviewed first.
- **low** FR-6 assumes one class per child (FR-6 "the child's class") — Students can be enrolled in several classes (calendar.md; `0016_class_enrollments.sql`). *Fix:* Say "class(es)" and state how the card shows several.

## Scope honesty — adequate
§6 Non-Goals does real work: notifications, a second parent, messaging, and profile edits are all excluded explicitly, and §4.1 has its own Out of Scope list. The Assumptions Index matches the inline tags exactly (10 inline, 10 indexed). There are 4 open questions, 10 assumptions and 0 `[NOTE FOR PM]` callouts, which is fine for an internal tool.

The largest omission is silent: moving from v1 to this increment. Students already approved under v1 registered with the old flow. Their `guardian_email` is retained (`0006_guardian_email_verification.sql`), some may be null, and none has a Parent. The PRD never says whether they link automatically when a matching Parent is approved, must link some other way, or stay unlinked. Under FR-8 ("the Student-side control is removed") and FR-9, an unlinked existing student cannot announce any leave, so every absence consumes grace. The FR-2 assumption "no Student can exist yet to match against" is false for these students.

### Findings
- **critical** Existing v1 students have no transition path (§3 Linked Child, FR-2, FR-4, FR-8) — Approved students with no Parent lose their leave control, and their streaks are penalised by their parent's non-adoption. It is also not stated whether a newly approved Parent automatically links to existing students whose stored `guardian_email` matches, or what happens to students whose `guardian_email` is null or wrong. The Glossary claim "a Student has exactly one Parent" is false on release day. *Fix:* Add an FR "Existing students": auto-link on Parent approval by matching `guardian_email`; the Admin can see unlinked students and correct their Guardian Email; until a student is linked, either the Student keeps the leave control or leave rules fall back to v1. Correct the FR-2 assumption, since the Admin *can* match against existing students.
- **high** Teacher/Admin who is also a parent is not addressed (FR-1, §6) — Volunteer teachers are likely to be parents at the school, and emails must be unique in Supabase Auth (addendum). The PRD neither supports nor excludes a Teacher account and a Parent account with the same email, or one account holding both roles. *Fix:* Decide and state it, either as a Non-Goal ("a Teacher who is a parent registers the Parent account with a different email") or as an FR.
- **high** Sick status may expose health information to classmates (FR-8, FR-10, §8) — FR-8 makes the answer visible to "classmates, and the child's Team". If Sick shows as Sick, peers see a minor's health status, which conflicts with the "minimize data on minors" constraint that §8 invokes for reasons only. *Fix:* State that peers see Sick as "On leave", and only Teachers, the Admin and the Parent see the Sick distinction.
- **medium** FR-1 and FR-2 conflict on re-registration (FR-1, FR-2) — "A Parent Email already used by another Parent account is refused" against "A rejected Parent can submit a new registration with the same email". *Fix:* State that rejection removes the account, or that the refusal covers only Pending/approved accounts.
- **medium** Password recovery is left as an open question for non-technical users (§10 Q4, FR-12) — A forgotten-password flow is table stakes for the persona "Non-technical; uses a family phone". Leaving it open risks shipping without it. *Fix:* Add it to FR-12 (reuse the existing Teacher reset flow if one exists), or mark it `[NON-GOAL for MVP]` with the Admin fallback spelled out.
- **low** Parent lifecycle for the Admin (listing, deactivating) is not specified (§10 Q3) — Only approval is covered. *Fix:* One consequence under FR-2: the Admin can see all Parents and their Linked Children, and can deactivate one.
- **low** The FR-3 error message allows email enumeration (FR-3) — "no parent account was found" tells any visitor whether an adult's email is a registered Parent. For an internal tool this is acceptable but should be a conscious choice. *Fix:* Note it as accepted risk, or add the Teacher-approval guard already cited in FR-3 as the mitigation.

## Downstream usability — adequate
There is a Glossary, the FR IDs run FR-1–FR-12 without gaps, UJ-1–UJ-3 each have a named protagonist (Dolma) with context inline, and the SMs name the FRs they validate. §5 is especially useful for a spec update downstream. What drags it down is terminology drift in §4.3 (above), two broken section references, and an addendum that partly contradicts the current decision.

### Findings
- **medium** §5 misses several contract clauses the PRD overrides (§5) — Not listed: the `roles-and-permissions.md` role table and its line "Parent view is a deferred future phase"; the calendar.md layer table ("Attendance intent | Student (own, per session)"); the SPEC Constraint "Student registration requires … a confirmed guardian email"; the SPEC Assumption "Leave protects a streak only when set before the session starts", which Sick Leave now breaks; and the SPEC Open Question on date-range leave, which UJ-3's multi-week trip makes relevant. *Fix:* Add these to §5 so the spec update is complete.
- **low** The addendum is partly stale (addendum.md bullet 2) — "the PRD only requires that registrations and parent signup work in any order" contradicts parent-first FR-3. Bullet 3 corrects it, but a reader of the architecture could act on bullet 2. *Fix:* Strike or rewrite bullet 2.

## Shape fit — adequate
The capability-spec shape suits an internal tool. Three short UJs with one named protagonist are right-sized, the SMs are partly operational (SM-3), and the length (about 3,000 words, about 6 pages) is within the agreed 5–8 pages. As a brownfield PRD, though, its references to existing behaviour must be accurate. The FR-2 assumption, the missing transition for existing students, and the gaps in §5 show it was written partly as if greenfield.

### Findings
- **medium** Brownfield accuracy (FR-2 assumption, §5) — See the critical finding under Scope honesty. The fix there also fixes this dimension.

## Mechanical notes
- **Broken cross-references:** §0 says inferred items are "indexed in §10", but the Assumptions Index is §11. §8 says "GDPR obligations stay open (§9)", but Open Questions are §10.
- **Glossary drift:** "pending" (Child Summary) vs "open" (FR-6/FR-7) Homework; "Pending" also used for account state. Session Leave is defined as Coming / On leave, but Sick is used as a third value. "Sick Leave" is also written as "Sick" (FR-10), "mark … Sick" (UJ-3).
- **UJ-3 vs FR-9 boundary:** "more than two weeks ahead" (UJ-3) vs "at least the Leave Notice Period" (FR-9, Glossary). Minor, but use one phrasing.
- **Assumptions roundtrip:** clean. All 10 inline `[ASSUMPTION]` tags appear in §11 and vice versa. Two inline tags have no stated content (FR-6, FR-10 "No reason…"); the index gives them wording, so this is fine.
- **IDs:** FR-1–FR-12, UJ-1–UJ-3, SM-1–SM-3 and SM-C1 are contiguous and unique. §4.4 and §4.5 have no Description, unlike §4.1–§4.3. This is fine given their size.
- **Required sections:** all present for the stakes. There is no Acceptance section, and none is needed because the FR consequences carry it.
